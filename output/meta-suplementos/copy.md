# Meta Suplementos

Approved copy, 22 October 2026. Presentation draft only.

**Role**

UX/UI + AI Art Direction · E-commerce

**Summary**

A supplement store redesign, from shopping flows to product imagery.

**Overview**

UX/UI redesign of the Meta Suplementos online store, informed by market and persona research. I redesigned every screen, from product discovery and catalogue browsing to product details and cart, with close attention to typography, spacing and component consistency. I also created AI-generated imagery featuring models and products, directing cohesive scenes across the storefront.

**The challenge**

Rethink an existing supplement store, bringing product discovery, detailed information and purchasing into a cohesive shopping experience.

**Design decisions**

I paired warm neutral backgrounds and serif typography with distinct colours for each product. Shared components keep the store consistent, while navigation by product and wellness goal offers different ways to explore the range. Product pages organise purchase options, ingredients, usage guidance and customer reviews into a clear hierarchy.

**Image direction**

I used AI as part of a professional image production workflow to create realistic scenes featuring models and products, directing lighting, colour and composition across the campaign banners. Each supplement has its own visual setting within a shared brand direction.
