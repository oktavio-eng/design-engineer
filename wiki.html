Transition plan, Otavio · GOW Studio · July 2026.

The principle

None of the names below got there by credential. All of them got there by public proof: a lib, a product, a manual, a site that speaks for itself. Portfolio > diploma. Building in public > climbing the ladder in silence. The whole plan follows from that.

Changelog

Every change to this site is written down as it ships: what landed, when, and why it was worth doing. Building in public only counts if the record is public too, so the log is part of the proof rather than a footnote to it. Read the changelog.

The plan

01

Foundation, now

  • Study the canon (below) in order: Rauno → Emil → Jakub → Josh.
  • Practice code beyond Framer: plain React, motion, modern CSS (OKLCHPerceptually uniform color space, more predictable than RGB/HSL for building palettes and adjusting tones., text-wrap, springsAnimation based on spring physics instead of fixed time curves. Feels more natural.).
  • Keep going with Design Circuit; evaluate mentorship with Willian Matiola with a specific goal: deciding which project becomes the public proof.
02

Public proof

  • Pick 1 project and open it up: Framer overrides as an open-source lib, or a technical write-up in English of a real system (Caderno de Erros, J3F).
  • Personal site in the group's style: white, typographic, no decoration, precise micro-interactions. This document is the aesthetic's prototype.
  • Publish in public: X, GitHub. The work pre-validates before any interview.
03

Positioning

  • Apply as mid-level remote, not junior. The level is already there: full handoffHanding off a design ready for implementation, with specs, assets, and behavior documented., production, API, a real squad.
  • Title: design engineer, not product designer. It shifts the recruiter's salary bracket.
  • Resume in English curated for this (base: Lupa's resume, B2B/SaaSBusiness-to-business and Software-as-a-Service: product sold to companies, billed by subscription. projects).
04

Inside the company

  • Actively hunt for mentorship: pair sessions, requested code review, get close to the most senior design engineer on the team.
  • Network in parallel starting now: contribute to the group's open source, comment, show up. That's how this market notices people.
  • Earn in dollars, stay in Brazil. Relocation becomes plan B, not a prerequisite.

Discarded: a design degree abroad. High cost, weak return in this niche, and it contradicts the goal of staying in Brazil. Only comes back on the table if the goal becomes moving abroad — and then by visa, not by portfolio.

Their aesthetic, decoded

White background. One font, one size. Hierarchy through weight, color, and space, never decoration. No icons, no cover images. The site can stand on text alone because spacing and rhythm carry it. Subtle, interruptible micro-interactions. It's the hardest skill on display with nowhere to hide, which is exactly why it works as proof.
