Changelog

What shipped on this site, newest first.

  1. Aug 7

    Dark mode, and the tools behind the scenes

    A theme toggle that follows the OS live until you override it, with no flash on load — the choice is read and applied before any stylesheet paints. Alongside it, a Storybook MVP: a visual inventory for development only, not a rewrite of the site into components.

  2. Aug 6

    Nineteen more names

    The people, references, and courses lists grew: 19 more people, 3 craft references, a new course entry. A tag-pill experiment on those rows got reverted back to plain text summaries after it broke horizontal scroll on mobile.

  3. Aug 5

    Flat typography, on trial

    An experiment collapsing every heading and paragraph to one size and one weight, testing whether gray, space, and rhythm alone can carry the whole hierarchy. Fully revertible — remove one stylesheet link and it's gone. Alongside it, hovering a list now dims every row but the one under the cursor.

  4. Aug 2–4

    Hello, in six languages

    An Apple-style intro greeting plays once per tab before the homepage appears, cutting between languages on a metronome and landing on the mark. It shipped at 20 languages and got trimmed four times chasing the right runtime and feel — 7, then 6, then 5 — plus a Safari bug fix that had kept it from playing at all.

  5. Aug 1

    Recovered, split into files, tokenized

    The starting point: the site recovered from production, split from one HTML file into markup, stylesheet, and script, a nonexistent browser storage API removed, and — by the end of the day — every color, timing, spacing, type, and radius value pulled out into its own token file.
